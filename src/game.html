SHIT STOMPERS

a silly little word game

To play

Tap the stinkers to spell a word, then STOMP it. Three letters or more. Enter stomps, Space shakes, Backspace wipes.

To win

Stomp every word on the list before the clock runs out. Hidden bonus words are icing — they score, but you don’t need them.

To score

20 points a letter at skill 1, 30 at 2, 40 at 3. The golden word pays 100 extra. The 4th word and the last word each pay a pile bonus. Rude words drop 25¢ in the swear jar — it costs you nothing.

Skill level sets the clock and the board: 2:30 with 12 words, 2:15 with 16, or 2:00 with 22. Your own dictionary — no weird Scrabble words on the list, and no word filter either. Round 1 is always the same starter rack.
