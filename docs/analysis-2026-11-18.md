# Shit Stompers — full audit and ideas, 2026-11-18

Written after a top-to-bottom read of `src/game.html` (1,854 lines), browser
play-throughs at desktop and 360×640, 360 sampled boards, and a round of
research into how other six-letter word games pace themselves. Bugs found during
the audit are fixed and listed below; ideas are parked, not built.

## How it was checked

- Every block of `src/game.html` read: CSS in order, then profanity, sound,
  music, voice, dictionary, round setup, play, timer, end, wire-up, title.
- Played rounds to completion through the real UI (clicking tiles, stomping
  words) on the starter rack and on random racks at all three skill levels.
- Sampled 120 boards per level from `generateRack()` + `boardWordsForLevel()` and
  asserted: never over the level's cap, no duplicates, nothing outside the rack,
  golden word always present.
- Measured layout at 390×844 and 360×640, and startup cost from
  `PerformanceNavigationTiming`.
- Console watched throughout: no errors.

## Bugs found and fixed

| # | Bug | Why it mattered | Fix |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | `idleSaid` never reset, so "Anybody there?" fired once per page load | A quiet player got encouraged exactly once, ever | Reset in `setupRound` |
| 2 | The countdown kept running while `document.hidden` | A phone call or locked screen killed the round | Timer holds while hidden and resets `lastWordAt`; measured 2.3s hidden = 0s lost |
| 3 | Golden word appended after the board cap: skill 3 said 24 and dealt 25 | Board size is a promise the HUD and rules both make | Evicts the least obvious chosen word (never the stem it brings) |
| 4 | `onSwearWord` replaced the points line | You never saw what a rude word scored | Appends: `ASS — +60  😈 SWEARY BONUS! Jar's at $0.25.` |
| 5 | Four-word racks paid the 4th-word and last-word bonuses to one pile but labelled it once | Thin-rack rounds underpaid or looked wrong | Combined label `+20+50` |
| 6 | Title card ran past the fold at 360×640; START sat 27px below the screen | First-time player could not see how to start | Combined `(max-width:640px) and (max-height:720px)` block at the end of the sheet |

Bug 6 had a cause worth remembering: `@media (max-width:640px)` comes *after*
`@media (max-height:720px)` in the sheet, so short-screen rules touching
`.hero-wrap`, `.logo` or `.tag` were silently overridden. Three attempted trims
did nothing before that was spotted.

## Known, deliberately not touched

- `bonusSet` is assigned and never read. `ROUND_SECONDS` is only an initial value
  that `setupRound` always overwrites. Both harmless, both dead.
- The board's answer words exist in the DOM as plain text (`color:transparent`).
  Invisible to a player, readable by select-all and by a screen reader.
- `gameTotal` resets on reload; only `ss_best` and the jar survive. A refresh
  wipes the run, not the record.
- `onSwearWord` is Jason's hook and is left as his to extend.

## How the genre does it

| Game | Letters | Targets | Clock | Required to pass |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| TextTwist | 6 | ~20 | 2:00 | just the all-letters word |
| Word Whomp | 6 | all on board | 2:00–2:30 | none; +15s for the highlighted word |
| Arkadium Scramble Words | 6–7 | all targets | ~60–90s per level | all targets, plus bonus words |
| Wordscapes | 4–7 | 10.3 avg (2–20) | **none** | grid slots + challenge word |
| Word Cookies | 3–9 | 20.4 avg (2–34) | **none** | displayed slots |
| Bookworm Adventures (Mutant Words) | — | 3 | 60s | all 3 |

Sources: [TextTwist 2](https://www.mobygames.com/game/106845/texttwist-2),
[~20 targets](https://wordfinder.yourdictionary.com/blog/text-twist-gives-word-fans-a-fresh-way-to-play),
[Word Whomp](https://www.pogo.com/games/word-whomp),
[Arkadium](https://support.arkadium.com/en/support/solutions/articles/44002027607--scramble-words-how-to-play-tips-scoring),
[Wordscapes](https://peoplefun.helpshift.com/hc/en/6-wordscapes/section/65-how-to-play-wordscapes),
[Word Cookies stats](https://wordcookieanswer.com/faq),
[~2-minute round guidance](https://mobilefreetoplay.com/bible/creating-habit-forming-session-design).

Takeaways: 16–24 targets is in line with the genre; ~7–9s per required word sits
inside the 6–9s band the timed games use; every one of them runs a curated
common-word list and treats obscure finds as optional — which is exactly this
game's board-words plus secret-stinkers split.

## Ideas, in the order I would build them

1. **Relaxed mode (no clock), or auto-pause.** Wordscapes and Word Cookies, the
   two biggest casual hits in the genre, are completely untimed. Laura is not a
   gamer. `timeForLevel` returning a large number, the clock hidden, and the
   hurry/idle lines skipped would cover it.
2. **Secret stinkers buy hints.** Arkadium's model: each bonus word fills a hint
   bar; at N finds, reveal a letter of a missing word (Word Whomp nudges the same
   way). Turns the "icing" bonus into a strategy and rescues a stuck player
   without touching the clock.
3. **Name the skill levels** (Easy / Regular / Spicy) with one line on what
   changes. Arrows plus a digit tell a new player nothing.
4. **Golden word pays +15s** as well as +100 points — exactly Word Whomp's rule,
   and the cheapest way to make skill 3 humane.
5. **Daily rack.** Seed the shuffle from the date: one rack a day, same for
   everyone, offline, no server, no accounts.
6. **Career total and the porch that changes with progress** (already parked in
   `ideas-and-decisions.md`). Needs `gameTotal` persisted first.
7. **Recap the near misses.** The reveal already greys missed words; add "you
   also had NOISE" for the three easiest ones, so a round teaches something.
8. **Structure the startup.** 130 ms of the 662 ms is the anagram index; it could
   be built after first paint with a "dealing the rack" beat on the title, which
   also gives the fly-in something to land on.
